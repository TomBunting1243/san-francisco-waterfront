# Waterfront reference audit

The landscape pass began on September 21, 2026. The architecture and remembered waterfront pass was updated on November 18, 2026. The scene combines mapped geography with architectural interpretation and places several remembered establishments alongside the newer ferry terminal. Photographs guide the modeled geometry and do not ship as runtime assets.

## Reconstructing the view from the Ferry Building

The model uses 0.06 scene units per metre. Earlier versions mixed independently sized props with metre-based footprints and let regional terrain extend underneath open piers. Orbiting or magnifying exposed intersections that were hidden in the opening frame. Shared dimensions, shoreline cutouts, and checks against the rendered geometry now cover those cases.

## Map and landscape evidence

`public/data/sf-waterfront.json` supplies mapped footprints and available building heights. Heights marked unmeasured remain estimates. `public/data/waterfront-landscape.json` adds an OpenStreetMap snapshot captured on September 21: 58 landscape areas, 800 path segments and 332 mapped tree nodes. These counts describe the input snapshot. The model filters underground routes, collisions and areas outside its parks.

Source: [OpenStreetMap map API](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=-122.402,37.79,-122.387,37.807). Both derived datasets are © OpenStreetMap contributors under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). No editor names or account metadata are exported.

The importer is reproducible with Python's standard library:

```sh
python3 scripts/import-waterfront-landscape.py --input /path/to/cached-osm-map.json --captured 2026-09-21
```

Omit `--input` to fetch the public map API. The script fits the existing scene projection against four mapped footprints and rejects a maximum alignment error above 0.003 scene units. This snapshot's maximum is approximately 0.001. Negative IDs in the garden module identify interpreted supplemental planting, not surveyed OSM nodes.

## Hills Bros. coffee plant and Infinity street fronts

The coffee plant follows the [San Francisco Planning Certificate of Appropriateness packet](https://commissions.sfplanning.org/hpcpackets/2011.0417A.pdf#page=20), sheets 18 and 20–22. The waterfront elevation uses a ground-floor arcade with recessed infill and tall sash, three rows of paired rectangular steel windows under paired round heads, a projecting corbel course and a flat gravel roof behind the parapet. The Folsom-side Hills Plaza screen has walk-through arches and a stepped entry, separate from the glazed plant wall. The two existing mapped tower outlines retain their elevations and have brick caps and upper vents. Pier depth, terrace approach and joint dimensions are visual estimates. The original neon lettering stays visible at its roof-edge support position.

The two formerly unnamed building outlines at [318 Spear Street](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/191861045) and [333 Main Street](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/421816058) receive the eight- and nine-floor cadence listed by [the Infinity owners association](https://infinitysf.org/infinity-facts/). Pale piers, recessed green-blue panes, plain entry hoods and limited projecting balconies use the [streetscape team's photographs](https://www.petersenstudio.com/portfolio/the-infinity) as a visual guide. Individual residential units, corridors and tenant arrangements are generalized.

## South Beach to China Basin

The continuous shoreline, wharf outline, marina centerlines, vessel slips, China Basin, park, ballpark outlines and surrounding street fronts derive from the [OpenStreetMap snapshot](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map.json?bbox=-122.399,37.7755,-122.383,37.7892). The imported shoreline includes the bay-facing coast through McCovey Cove and inner Mission Creek. The map's seven main marina dock labels and the fishing access remain separately identifiable. Water raycasts check that the wharf and field are supported and the marina, cove and creek remain open. The [Port pier survey](https://www.sfport.com/projects-programs/south-beach-piers-38-40) and [Bay Water Trail’s Pier 40 profile](https://sfbaywatertrail.org/trailhead/pier-40/) inform the waterfront's apron, shed and marina shapes. Boat positions are representative of occupied berths.

The field, stadium shell and gates are located from the mapped features. The [Giants' stadium guide](https://www.mlb.com/news/featured/oracle-park-guide-capacity-seating-chart-parking-and-more) and [ballpark attractions](https://www.mlb.com/giants/ballpark/attractions) inform the bowl, right-field arcade, steel light towers, cove edge, bottle, glove and public gate lettering. The model uses grouped seating tiers and simplified secondary façades. The Willie Mays Gate and the Mays and McCovey statue locations follow the supplied map. The scene uses small bronze-colored gestures and pedestals to mark William Behrends’s sculptures. [Giants media guide, Statue Dedications](https://pressbox.athletics.com/Publications/MLB%20Media%20Guides/Archives/2021%20Media%20Guides/2021%20San%20Francisco%20Giants%20Media%20Guide.pdf)

## Remaining fidelity limits

- This does **not** establish that every visible building matches every real elevation. Unnamed inland/backdrop blocks still use mapped extrusions and generic window grids. The Steuart-side elevation at 139 Steuart and the other parcels at Battery/Green are still approximate.
- Façades repeated around a footprint, colors, roof equipment, tree crowns and many street furnishings are artistic interpretations. Night window occupancy is a fixed artistic pattern, not live occupancy data. Exact masonry courses, tenancy signs and hidden façades have not been surveyed.
- Park geometry combines mapped paths/planting and photographed landscape character. It is not a complete current tree or sculpture census. Dates differ across the references; proposed future landscaping is excluded.
- The geometry and animation checks cover the specific invariants above. Frame rate, GPU memory, thermal behavior and continuous visual quality require separate rendered measurements.

For future work, identify the exact mapped building first, inspect an existing-condition exterior photo or plan, record its distinctive features here, and then update its geometry. Do not turn an anonymous block into a plausible but unsupported landmark.

The [Catalina 315](https://www.catalinayachts.com/cruiser-series/catalina-315/) and [Oceanis 34.1](https://www.beneteau.com/oceanis/oceanis-341) inform the larger boats’ beam, coachroof and rig proportions.
