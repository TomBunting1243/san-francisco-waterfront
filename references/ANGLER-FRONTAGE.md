# Angler at 132 The Embarcadero

The 14.08 m frontage occupies the waterfront edge of building way 193054137. The mapped footprint overlaps San Francisco parcel 3715/025 by 98.9%. The building model retains its local 17.50 m roof elevation. The north and south party walls meet separate buildings. Modernist gives its street address as 139 Steuart on the opposite side of the block. [Mapped building](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/193054137) · [City parcel](https://data.sf.gov/resource/fdfd-xptc.json?mapblklot=3715025) · [Modernist](https://www.modernist.club/locations)

Jones Product’s drawn elevation shows three upper punched bays, a taller glazed upper band and three ground-floor openings. The operator’s 2024 photograph on page 12 shows the as-built main door. A dark wood revolving entrance occupies its right half, with a separate flat leaf on the left, suspended lettering in the transom, paired ribbed sconces, number 132 and small potted trees. Page 4 shows the lounge’s round tables, upholstered bench, copper bar front, exposed brick, pendant lights and mounted fish. Furniture spacing and the first 10 m of room depth are an interpretation of those views. [Design-team elevation and opening photographs](https://www.jonesproduct.com/angler-san-francisco) · [Saison Hospitality photographs, pp. 4 and 12](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5a82c9a58fd4d2d4199515f5/t/65e2411ed3463961ce0cf65a/1709326670024/Angler%2BEvents%2BDeck%2B2024%2B-%2B2.27.24.pdf)
