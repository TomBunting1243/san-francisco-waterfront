# Additional field and archive sources

This register preserves public citations from 25 building, transit and waterfront research records. Some record alternatives, source surveys or earlier interpretations. The waterfront audit describes the geometry that reached the model.

## Measured research inputs

The 2023 San Francisco LiDAR working set contained **1,359 LASzip octree chunks**, totaling **408,176,740 stored bytes**. Reading the LAS point count in each header gave **50,332,832 point records**. These are records across octree levels in one retained working set. The count does not establish unique physical returns. Eight retained California NAIP roof-imagery PNG extracts occupied another **31,686,916 bytes**. The municipal 2024 eight-centimeter orthophoto working set also retained **661 distinct zoom-20 tiles** totaling **75,062,728 bytes**, counting each public tile ID once. The three measured groups total **514,926,384 bytes**. The [survey asset register](SURVEY-ASSETS.csv) lists every recovered ID and public request in these groups. Their original working files are not part of the city download. The figure excludes source PDFs, separate surveys, other raster crops, tool environments, videos and rendering QA. [USGS 2023 survey index](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/CA_SanFrancisco_1_B23/ept.json) · [California NAIP imagery service](https://gis.wildlife.ca.gov/images/rest/services/Base_Remote_Sensing/NAIP_2022/ImageServer)

The historic west-of-Market composition reference has no retained publisher, photograph ID or public URL. The apparent figure beside the tents in that view has no confirmed identification. Some other references are identified only to an operator gallery, an archive PDF or an imagery-service layer. Crops of individual pages, screenshots for comparison and generated film frames have no separate claim to source-image authorship.

The rights to a referenced photograph or plan remain with its creator or publisher unless the item states otherwise. The research links identify the publicly accessible originals and their context.
