## Elevation data

Regional elevations were sampled from the Mapzen Terrarium elevation tiles. Source datasets include USGS National Elevation Dataset and NOAA bathymetry. The locally stored mesh requires no runtime tile service. See the source inventory and terms at https://www.mapzen.com/data/elevation/ and https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/.
